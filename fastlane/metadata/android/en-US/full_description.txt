Autka is an open-source Android project for car shopping across Poland, Europe and US imports. It combines connected lawful data feeds with deep links to original marketplaces, so the same search intent can continue even when Autka is not allowed to ingest a site's inventory.

Features
* Search, filter and sort the connected/cached catalogue by make, price, year, mileage, fuel, transmission, region and source.
* Continue a search on original Polish, European and US marketplace pages through deep links instead of scraping them.
* Estimate USA-to-Poland landed cost: shipping, customs duty, Polish excise and VAT, with engine size and drivetrain taken into account.
* Use the import calculator from a listing or as a standalone tool.
* Compare PLN, EUR and USD prices with NBP exchange rates and explicit stale-rate fallback.
* View listing locations on an OpenStreetMap-based map.
* Check public-safe source status and keep the last cached catalogue available offline.

Privacy
* No ads and no analytics SDKs.
* Android currently requests only internet and network-state permissions.

Current project status
Live catalogue coverage depends on licensed, partner-provided or seller-authorised feeds. Debug/demo sample offers are never enabled in production; unsupported marketplaces remain search deep-links until a compliant feed exists.

Autka is open source under Apache-2.0. Import-cost figures are estimates only; actual customs valuation, tax treatment, shipping and fees can differ.
